## What belongs in an article

The main pages — Start here, Costs, and the ones still being written — are the reference. An article is for one question that needs more room, written the same way: plain language, and no numbers this site cannot stand behind.

## What you can use on the page

Ordinary paragraphs, headings, and lists are enough. The same cards, tables, and closing box used on Costs are available when a piece needs them. This sample only uses a list and a closing box.

- Put the title, description, and date at the top of the file, between the `---` lines.
- Write the article underneath, the way this paragraph is written.
- Save the file. It shows up on the articles page, newest date first.
