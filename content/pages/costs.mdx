## Where a season’s money goes

<Cards>

<Card title="Team fees" label="The flyer number" id="team-fees">

Club or travel dues, league fees, and the “uniform package.” This is the part everyone sees first.

<Ask>Ask what’s actually included: how many jerseys, and whether tournament entries are extra.</Ask>

</Card>

<Card title="Travel" label="Often the biggest surprise" id="travel">

Hotels, gas, meals on the road, parking, and sometimes a separate tournament entry. Hotels are usually the part families don’t see coming.

A team that plays most weekends away can double or triple the flyer number without anyone calling it a fee increase.

</Card>

<Card title="Gear" label="Beyond what the team orders" id="gear">

Shoes or cleats, bags, sport-specific equipment, and replacements as kids grow, sometimes mid-season.

Some programs require a specific brand, a second uniform, or extra training gear.

</Card>

<Card title="The extras nobody lists" label="Small, then not small" id="extras">

Fundraising (or the buyout if you’d rather not sell), team photos, banquets, required lessons or extra skills sessions, coach gifts, and tournaments added late in the season.

Then it all happens again for a second child on a roster.

</Card>

</Cards>

## Where the money goes, side by side

<Compare>

| Cost | What it usually covers | On the flyer? | Why it sneaks up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Team fees | Dues, league or club fees, a basic uniform package | Usually, at least the main number | “Included” can be narrower than it sounds; tournament entries and extra uniforms are often separate |
| Travel | Hotels, gas, meals, parking, sometimes tournament entry | Rarely | It’s spread across many weekends, so no single bill looks big, and the total is often larger than the registration fee |
| Gear | Equipment, shoes, bags, training gear, replacements | Sometimes partly, if there’s a required team order | Kids grow, gear wears out, and required brands or extras show up after you’ve committed |
| Extra obligations | Fundraising or buyouts, photos, banquets, lessons, gifts, added events | Rarely | Each one feels small and optional, and they arrive one at a time |

<Note>Every program is different. Use this as a checklist of questions, not a price list.</Note>

</Compare>

## How to add it up before you commit

<Checklist>

- Ask for a full-season cost estimate in writing, not just the registration fee.
- Count the away weekends and the hotel nights they’ll likely need.
- Ask whether tournament fees are included or billed separately.
- Ask about fundraising requirements, and whether there’s a buyout.
- Add any gear your child doesn’t already own or will likely outgrow.
- If more than one child plays, add each schedule up separately; the weekends may not overlap.

</Checklist>

</AddUp>
