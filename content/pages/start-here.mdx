## The four words you’ll hear most

<Cards>

<Card title="Rec" label="Recreational" id="rec">

Usually a local league run through a city, a Y, or a parks and rec department. Kids are often placed by age or a light evaluation, not a high-stakes tryout.

Seasons are shorter, travel is mostly across town, costs are the lowest of these options, and playing time is more even.

<Ask>The default starting point for most families.</Ask>

</Card>

<Card title="Travel" label="Leaves town to compete" id="travel">

A team that regularly leaves town for tournaments or games. Tryouts are common. The season can run longer, weekends get spoken for, and costs jump because of tournament fees, hotels, gas, and extra gear.

“Travel ball” is the usual phrase in baseball and softball; other sports may just say a team “travels.”

</Card>

<Card title="Club" label="An organized program" id="club">

Often year-round or multi-season, with paid coaches, regular training, and a pathway through age groups. Soccer, volleyball, basketball, and lacrosse use “club” a lot.

Club can look a lot like travel: tryouts, higher fees, more hours. Some clubs also field rec or developmental teams.

<Ask>Ask what this specific team actually does.</Ask>

</Card>

<Card title="Showcase" label="Usually an event, not a team" id="showcase">

The pitch is exposure: college coaches, recruiting profiles, measured times and skills. Families pay entry fees and travel for a weekend, or a series of them.

Some showcases are worthwhile; others mainly collect fees. A showcase is not a substitute for a good regular program, and it’s rarely the right first step for a younger child.

</Card>

</Cards>

## Side by side

<Compare>

| Type | Commitment | How you join | Typical cost level | Best starting point for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rec | One season at a time; practices and games close to home | Sign up; placed by age or a light evaluation | Lowest | Most kids, especially first-timers and younger players |
| Travel | Longer seasons; many weekends away | Tryout, sometimes an invite | High — fees plus tournaments, hotels, gas, gear | Kids who love the sport and want more, with a family ready for the schedule |
| Club | Often year-round or multi-season; regular training | Tryout or evaluation; some clubs also have open rec teams | High — varies by club and team level | Kids ready for structured training and a longer-term program |
| Showcase | A weekend or a series of events | Register and pay per event; some are invite-only | Per event — entry fees plus travel | Older players with a specific recruiting goal, alongside a regular team |

<Note>These are general patterns. Leagues and clubs use the words differently, so always ask what a specific team means by them.</Note>

</Compare>

Before you pay a deposit, talk it through as a family:

<Checklist>

- **Time:** what weekdays and weekends this will take, for your child and for everyone who drives.
- **Money:** the full season, not just the registration number on the flyer.
- **Whose idea it is:** whether your child is asking for this, or the adults are.

</Checklist>
